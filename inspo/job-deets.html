About the Role

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join Spotify's Creator Tools team. You'll lead the design of features that help millions of artists share music, connect with fans, and grow their audience on the world's largest streaming platform.

You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and data scientists to ship high-impact work. This is a role for someone who thrives in ambiguity, loves systems thinking, and cares deeply about craft at scale.

Requirements
  • 6+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years at a scaled consumer product
  • Deep expertise in design systems — building, maintaining, and evolving component libraries
  • Strong portfolio showing end-to-end product thinking from discovery to shipped features
  • Experience with data-informed design — working with A/B tests, user research, and analytics
  • Proficiency in Figma with advanced prototyping and auto-layout skills
  • Excellent communication — can present work to leadership and influence cross-functional stakeholders
  • Bonus: experience with motion design, front-end development, or accessibility standards
