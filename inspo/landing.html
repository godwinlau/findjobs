You have questions. We have answers.
Here's what everyone asks right before they realize they should've signed up 5 minutes ago.
Wait, this is actually free?
+
Free as in "free free." Not free-trial-free or free-but-we'll-upsell-you-free. 100% free for job seekers, forever. Employers pay to post and access our matching. You never pay a peso. We promise. Pinky swear.
How is this different from JobStreet or LinkedIn?
+
They throw 500 listings at you and wish you luck. We score your skills against every job and only show matches where you'd actually get hired. Plus transparent PHP salaries (not "competitive") and a skill gap feature that tells you exactly what to learn next. It's like going from Google Maps to a personal driver.
Are these real jobs or are you catfishing me?
+
Every single job is verified. We pull from company career pages, partner APIs, and direct employer postings. No fake listings, no duplicates, no expired roles from 2023 that somehow still appear (looking at you, every other platform). If it's on Bigmovv, you can apply today.
I'm not in Manila. Am I invisible here too?
+
Absolutely not. 62% of our jobs are remote. We have people hired from Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Cagayan de Oro, and everywhere in between. Your location doesn't determine your talent. Your score does.
What does my score actually mean?
+
It's the percentage of a job's required and preferred skills that match your profile. Score 92% and you match 92% of what the employer wants. Above 85% and you're a strong candidate. We also show you which skills would bump your score higher — so you know exactly what to learn next, not just what you're missing.
