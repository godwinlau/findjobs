// honest answers
Still not sure?
Fair. Here are the questions everyone asks before signing up.
Is this actually free?
+
Yes. Completely free for job seekers. No hidden fees, no premium tier, no credit card required. We make money from employers who pay to post jobs and access our matching platform. You'll never pay a single peso.
How is this different from JobStreet or LinkedIn?
+
Those platforms show you everything and let you sort through thousands of listings. We do the opposite — we analyze your skills and only show you jobs where you're a strong match. Plus we show salaries in PHP upfront, give you a match score for every job, and tell you exactly which skills to learn to unlock more opportunities.
Are these real jobs from real companies?
+
Every job on our platform is verified. We aggregate from company career pages, partner APIs, and direct employer postings. No fake listings, no duplicates, no expired roles. If a job is on Jobboard, you can apply to it today.
I'm outside Manila. Will this work for me?
+
Absolutely. 62% of our jobs are remote-friendly, so it doesn't matter if you're in Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, or anywhere else. We've had users hired from across the Philippines. Our community section shows real people from cities all over the country.
What if I don't have many skills yet?
+
That's exactly who our Skills Intelligence feature is for. We'll show you which skills are in demand, which ones would unlock the most jobs for you, and link you to free resources to learn them. Start with what you have — we'll help you grow.
